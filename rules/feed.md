# watched refs, newest push first

Following shows the posts of the handles and paths you watch, newest push first. A push is ordered by its effective time: the earlier of its committer date and the moment diff.me first observed it, so a commit dated in the future does not sort above everything else, and history pushed late does not arrive as new.

A post appears once, at its newest version, and an amend brings it back to the top. The whole public corpus has its own rule, `rules/all.md`.

